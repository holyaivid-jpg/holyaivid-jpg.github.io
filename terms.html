Terms of Service – holyaivid Publisher

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. About the service

holyaivid Publisher ("the app") is a private desktop tool operated by Yannick Strek ("we"). It lets the operator publish their own videos to their own TikTok account using the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. The app is not offered to third parties.

2. Use of TikTok

By connecting a TikTok account you also agree to TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. Only content the user owns or has the rights to may be published. Videos created with AI are labeled as AI-generated.

3. User control

The user chooses the caption, privacy level and interaction settings and confirms every post before it is sent to TikTok. The user can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services) or in the tool itself.
