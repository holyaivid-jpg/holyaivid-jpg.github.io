Privacy Policy – holyaivid Publisher

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Yannick Strek · strekyannick@gmail.com

2. What data the app uses

  • From TikTok (with your authorization): your open ID, username, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and the permission to upload and publish videos (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, so the app can upload videos at the scheduled time.
  • Your videos and captions that you choose to publish.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to show which TikTok account is connected and to upload and publish the videos you selected. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. There is no advertising and no tracking.

4. Where the data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only locally on the operator's own computer. Videos are sent directly to TikTok via the official TikTok API. No external servers or databases are used.

5. How long data is kept – deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services) or via the tool's logout function, which also deletes the stored tokens. Published videos are managed in your TikTok account and subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights

You can request information about, correction or deletion of your data at any time by email to strekyannick@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
