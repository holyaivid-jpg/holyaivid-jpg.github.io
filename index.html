holyaivid Publisher

holyaivid Publisher is a private desktop tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @holyaivid to publish their own short videos to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator logs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The tool shows the connected TikTok account, the video, the caption and lets the creator choose the privacy level and interaction settings.
  3. After the creator confirms, the video is uploaded via the TikTok Content Posting API at the scheduled time.

The tool is not offered to the public and is only used for the creator's own account. All videos are labeled as AI-generated content.
